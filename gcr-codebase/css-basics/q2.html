Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City. It is famous for its royal palaces, forts, colorful markets and rich culture.

History of Jaipur

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. It was one of India's earliest planned cities. The city is known for its beautiful architecture and historical monuments.

Famous Places

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is one of Jaipur's most famous landmarks, known for its unique windows.

Amber Fort

Amber Fort is a magnificent historical fort located on a hill near Jaipur.

City Palace

City Palace is a beautiful royal complex containing museums and courtyards.

Culture of Jaipur

Jaipur is famous for Rajasthani music, dance, traditional clothing, handicrafts and delicious local food. Festivals add vibrant colors to the city's cultural life.
