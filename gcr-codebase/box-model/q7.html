Why Typography Matters

Good typography makes written content easier to understand and more comfortable to read. A carefully chosen font family can give a page a professional, friendly, or modern appearance.

Sans-serif fonts are often used for modern interfaces because they look clean and simple. Different font sizes and weights can also create a clear visual hierarchy between headings, paragraphs, and important information.

Typography is not only about choosing a font. Proper spacing, alignment, and line height are equally important for creating a balanced reading experience.
